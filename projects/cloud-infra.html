Zero Single-Point-of-Failure

Dual transit tunnels engineered with active/standby BGP routing. Should an ISP fiber cut occur, Bidirectional Forwarding Detection (BFD) triggers automatic route withdrawal and reroutes traffic within 48 milliseconds.

Dynamic BGP Route Filtering

Route-maps and prefix-lists enforce strict ingress/egress filtering. On-premise only advertises RFC 1918 campus routes (`192.168.0.0/16`), preventing accidental route leaking to upstream transit providers.

Declarative Infrastructure (IaC)

VPCs, subnets, route tables, and transit gateway attachments are codified in Terraform. Reduces deployment drift, guarantees audit compliance, and allows full disaster recovery redeployment in under 4 minutes.
