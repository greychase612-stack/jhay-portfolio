Online Store Sales & Performance Analysis

A comprehensive data analysis case study executing raw data cleansing, performance visualization, and evidence-based strategic formulation using Microsoft Excel.

Dataset Overview & Cleansing

The analysis began with a raw e-commerce dataset containing 50 transaction rows. I performed data validation to ensure data integrity:

  • Verified exactly 50 total records.
  • Confirmed 0 missing or invalid values.
  • Confirmed 0 duplicate Order IDs.
  • Status: The data was clean and ready for analytical processing.

Major Patterns & Findings

Loyalty Dominates

Returning customers account for the vast majority of the revenue (nearly 59%) and maintain a noticeably higher average order value than new customers.

Payment Preferences

Traditional Bank transfers and Cash on Delivery (COD) are the dominant payment methods, significantly outperforming Cards and GCash in both transaction volume and total revenue.

Discount Inefficiency

The highest revenue tier comes from transactions with absolutely no discount (0%), while the 5% tier drives the most order volume. Steeper discounts (10% and 15%) are notably failing to incentivize proportionally higher total sales.

Product Dynamics

The "Software" category and individual "Webcams" serve as the primary revenue drivers, whereas "SSDs" are moving the highest physical unit volume out of the store.

Evidence-Based Recommendations

1. Enhance Retention Efforts

Since Returning Customers spend significantly more per transaction (Avg: ₱1,885.28 vs ₱1,555.54), the store must implement targeted post-purchase follow-ups or a loyalty system. The goal is to rapidly convert the 23 recorded New Customers into repeat buyers.

2. Optimize the Discount Strategy

Management should reevaluate the 10% and 15% discount tiers, as they consistently yield the lowest total sales. Those profit margins should be reallocated to heavily promote the highly successful 5% discount tier, which currently drives the highest overall transaction volume.

3. Cross-Sell High-Volume Components

Since SSDs are the most frequently purchased physical item (33 units sold) but Webcams drive the most overall revenue, the store should create "PC Upgrade" or "Work from Home" bundles. Combining SSDs with Webcams or Headsets will organically push average cart values higher.
